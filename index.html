How we work

A Florida company, and the rules stay with you

Zebedee Property LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We manage the operation, not the compliance. Short-term rental licensing, registration, HOA or condominium rules, and transient rental and sales tax obligations sit with the owner — they vary enormously between counties and cities, and they're worth professional advice before you list. We'll tell you when something looks like it needs checking.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do I need a licence to rent short term?

Often yes, and it depends on your city, county and sometimes your HOA. That's the owner's responsibility, and it's worth confirming before the first booking rather than after.

Are you a real estate brokerage?

No. We manage the rental operation. We don't sell property, represent anyone in a transaction, or give legal or tax advice.

What can you spend without asking me?

Whatever threshold we agree in writing — commonly a small amount for urgent repairs. Anything above it comes to you first.

How is your fee calculated?

Quoted per property and set out in the agreement before anything starts. No percentage appears later that wasn't agreed.

Can you guarantee occupancy?

No, and nobody honest can. Rates and occupancy depend on season, location and the market. We'll give you a realistic view rather than an optimistic one.

Who handles the tax on bookings?

Transient rental and sales tax obligations are the owner's. Platforms collect some of it in some places and not others — take advice on your specific situation.
